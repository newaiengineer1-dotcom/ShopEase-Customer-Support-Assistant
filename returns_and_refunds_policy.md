# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Return window
Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a refund to the original payment method.
Electronics such as phones, tablets, headphones and chargers can be returned within 15 days of delivery.
Items that arrive damaged or defective, or that are not what you ordered, can be returned within 30 days of delivery, including electronics.

## Items that cannot be returned
Perishable groceries, gift cards and digital downloads cannot be returned once they are delivered.

## Condition of returned items
Items should be unused and returned with their original packaging and accessories. A refund may be reduced if an item comes back used or with missing parts.

## How to start a return
Sign in, open Your Orders, choose the item and select Return or replace items. Pick a reason and a return method, then print the prepaid label or show the QR code at a drop-off point.

## Return shipping costs
Returns are free when the item is damaged, defective or not what you ordered, and for ShopEase Plus members. For other returns a $4.99 return label fee is deducted from the refund.

## When you receive your refund
After the returned item reaches our warehouse and passes inspection, the refund is issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. Your bank may need extra time to post it. Refunds paid to ShopEase Pay balance are available within 1 business day.

## Replacements
For a damaged or defective item you can choose a replacement instead of a refund when one is available. Replacements ship at no charge.
