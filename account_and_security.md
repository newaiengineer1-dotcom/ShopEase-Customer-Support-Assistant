# Account and Security

## Resetting your password
Select Forgot password on the sign-in page and enter your account email. We send a reset link that expires after 30 minutes.

## Two-step verification
Turn on two-step verification under Account > Login and security. ShopEase will never ask for your password, one-time passcode or full card number by chat, phone or email.

## Updating your details
Update addresses under Your Addresses and your email or phone under Login and security. Changing your email requires a one-time passcode sent to the new address.

## Suspicious activity
If you see an order you did not place, change your password, turn on two-step verification and contact support right away. We may temporarily lock the account while we review it.

## Talking to a human agent
Ask the assistant to connect you and it will open a support ticket. A human agent replies to tickets within 24 hours.
