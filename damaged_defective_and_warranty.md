# Damaged, Defective and Warranty

## Reporting a damaged or defective item
Report the problem within 30 days of delivery from Your Orders and attach at least one photo. We then offer a replacement or a refund, with free return shipping.

## Manufacturer warranty
Most electronics include a manufacturer warranty, typically 12 months. After the 30-day return period, warranty claims go directly to the manufacturer. The warranty terms are listed on the product page.

## ShopEase Protection Plan
The optional Protection Plan lasts 2 years and covers accidental damage such as drops and spills. It can be added within 30 days of buying an eligible product. Claims are filed from Your Orders.
