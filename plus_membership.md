# ShopEase Plus Membership

## Plans and pricing
ShopEase Plus costs $9.99 per month or $89 per year. New members can start with a 30-day free trial.

## Member benefits
- Free standard and expedited shipping on eligible items with no minimum order
- Free returns on most items, with no return label fee
- Early access to selected deals

## Cancelling your membership
You can cancel at any time under Account > Membership. Benefits continue until the end of the period you already paid for. If you cancel within 3 days after a renewal charge and have not used any member benefits since then, you can request a full refund of that charge.

## Free trial
Cancel before the 30-day trial ends to avoid being charged. When the trial ends the plan you selected starts automatically.
