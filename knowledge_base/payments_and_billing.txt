Payments and Billing

ACCEPTED PAYMENT METHODS
ShopEase accepts major credit and debit cards, ShopEase gift cards and ShopEase Pay balance. Cash on delivery is not available.

WHEN YOU ARE CHARGED
When you place an order your bank may show a temporary authorization hold. You are charged only when the order ships. If an order is cancelled or a payment fails, the hold is released within 3 to 5 business days.

FAILED PAYMENTS
If a payment fails, check the card number, expiry date and billing address, or try another payment method. Your order stays in Processing for 24 hours waiting for a successful payment and is cancelled automatically after that.

PROMOTIONAL CODES
One promotional code can be used per order and codes cannot be combined. A code that has expired cannot be reinstated.

INVOICES AND RECEIPTS
An invoice is available in Your Orders once the order has shipped. Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on the delivery address.
