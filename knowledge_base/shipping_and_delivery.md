# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery speeds and costs
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and is free on orders of $35 or more; smaller orders pay a $5.99 shipping fee.
Expedited shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $9.99.
ShopEase Plus members get free standard and expedited shipping on eligible items with no minimum order.

## Tracking your order
Open Your Orders and select Track package. A tracking number appears once the order has shipped, usually within 1 business day after you place it. The estimated delivery date updates automatically if the carrier reports a delay.

## Package shows delivered but you cannot find it
First check around your delivery location and ask neighbors, because carriers sometimes mark a package delivered early. If it has not arrived after 48 hours, contact support and we will open a carrier investigation. If the package cannot be located we will send a replacement or refund the order.

## Changing the delivery address
The delivery address can be changed only while the order status is Processing. After an order has shipped the address cannot be changed; you can refuse the delivery or return the items after they arrive.

## Delivery area
ShopEase currently delivers to addresses in the United States only.
