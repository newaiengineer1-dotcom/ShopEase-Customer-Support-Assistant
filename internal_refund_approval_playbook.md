# INTERNAL - Refund Approval Playbook (staff only)

## Approval limits
Support agents may approve refunds up to $150. Refunds above $150 need supervisor approval, and refunds above $500 need the Head of Support. Goodwill credits are capped at $20 per customer per quarter.

## Fraud signals
Escalate to the Trust team when a customer reports several undelivered packages within 30 days or asks for refunds to a different payment method.
